2026 County Observed Holidays

Manatee County government offices will be closed on the following observed holidays in 2026. This list represents the 11 holidays per the county HR policy; emergency services remain available.

Holidays

  • 2026-01-01: New Year's Day
  • 2026-01-19: MLK Day
  • 2026-02-16: Presidents Day
  • 2026-05-25: Memorial Day
  • 2026-07-03: Independence Day observed
  • 2026-09-07: Labor Day
  • 2026-11-11: Veterans Day
  • 2026-11-26: Thanksgiving
  • 2026-11-27: Day after Thanksgiving
  • 2026-12-24: Christmas Eve
  • 2026-12-25: Christmas Day

If a holiday falls on a Saturday, it is observed the previous Friday. If it falls on a Sunday, it is observed the following Monday. Working-day calculations exclude these dates.
