## Background

Synthetic Realty Partners III LLC filed Variance Request VAR-2024-0057 on
January 9, 2024, seeking relief from LDC §5.8(a)(2), which requires a minimum
25-foot rear setback in RSF-3 zoning districts. The applicant proposes
construction of a 2,400 sq. ft. single-family residence with an attached
lanai on a lot that is 87 feet deep — significantly shallower than the 110-foot
minimum depth for which the 25-foot rear setback was designed. The proposed
structure would encroach 7.5 feet into the required rear setback, leaving a
17.5-foot rear buffer.

The lot's unusually shallow configuration results from a 1987 Plat of Mockingbird
Hollow, recorded in Plat Book 41, Page 78, Manatee County Public Records,
which subdivided a larger parcel under an earlier zoning code that did not
impose current setback requirements. The Planning Department staff report
identifies this as a "pre-existing nonconforming lot of record" under LDC
§3.2(b).

## Legal Analysis

### Variance Standard under LDC §7.3(d)

The LDC requires satisfaction of five criteria for variance approval:

1. The variance is necessary due to special conditions and circumstances not
   resulting from the actions of the applicant.
2. Strict application of the regulations would result in peculiar and
   exceptional practical difficulties or exceptional undue hardship.
3. The variance will not impair light, air, or public health, safety, and welfare.
4. The variance is the minimum necessary to make reasonable use of the property.
5. The variance will not be contrary to the public interest.

### Criterion 1 — Special Circumstances

The lot's 87-foot depth is a function of the 1987 plat recorded under prior
county code, not of any act or choice of the current applicant. The 1987
plat predates the RSF-3 setback requirements enacted in the 1992 LDC revision.
Criterion 1 is satisfied on these facts. Staff's analysis at MC-401215 through
MC-401220 reaches the same conclusion.

### Criterion 2 — Hardship

A strict application of the 25-foot rear setback on an 87-foot-deep lot
leaves only 62 feet of buildable depth between the 25-foot front setback and
the 25-foot rear setback — after accounting for the required 5-foot side yards,
the maximum allowable footprint for a one-story structure would be approximately
1,550 sq. ft. Under current construction cost norms, the economics of
developing a residential lot at that footprint cap make the lot effectively
unbuildable for market-rate single-family housing. This office agrees with
staff that criterion 2 is satisfied, though the showing is fact-intensive and
could be challenged by an adjacent property owner.

### Criterion 3 — Light, Air, Health, Safety

The proposed 17.5-foot rear buffer exceeds the 15-foot rear setback that applies
in several adjacent zoning districts, and the neighboring lots to the rear face
in the same direction. The staff report (MC-401221 through MC-401225) identifies
no impact on public health, safety, or welfare. Criterion 3 is satisfied.

### Criterion 4 — Minimum Variance

The applicant originally requested a 10-foot encroachment (15-foot rear buffer).
Staff recommended reducing the requested variance to 7.5 feet during pre-application
conference, and the applicant amended its request accordingly. The revised
application is supported by a revised site plan that relocates the lanai
attachment to eliminate unnecessary encroachment. Criterion 4 appears satisfied.

### Criterion 5 — Public Interest

No objections have been received from adjacent property owners within the
notification radius, and the variance is consistent with similar variances
granted in Mockingbird Hollow in 2019 (VAR-2019-0041) and 2021 (VAR-2021-0033).
Criterion 5 appears satisfied.

## Settlement Posture

No adversarial proceeding is pending. This memorandum addresses the legal risk
profile of the Planning Commission's vote rather than a litigation posture.

[ATTORNEY-CLIENT PRIVILEGED] — The principal risk is a third-party standing
challenge by a neighboring property owner who objects to the variance but did
not submit written comments during the notification period. Under Florida
standing doctrine for zoning appeals, a neighboring owner within the notification
radius who did not participate in the original proceeding generally lacks
standing to challenge the variance grant. The two neighbors who did respond
to notification letters expressed no objection. Legal risk of a successful
challenge is assessed as low.

## Analogous Matters

This application is factually analogous to VAR-2021-0033 (Mockingbird Hollow,
resolved 2021-09), in which a 7-foot rear encroachment was approved on an
86-foot-deep lot with similar pre-existing plat conditions. The Planning
Commission's approval in VAR-2021-0033 was not challenged. It is distinguishable
from VAR-2022-0108 (denied 2022-11), where the applicant created the
nonconforming condition by voluntarily subdividing a conforming parcel.

## Recommendation

[ATTORNEY-CLIENT PRIVILEGED] — Counsel finds no legal basis to recommend
denial of VAR-2024-0057. The five criteria under LDC §7.3(d) are met on these
facts, and the legal risk of a post-approval challenge is low. If the Planning
Commission approves the variance, counsel recommends including a condition that
the 17.5-foot rear buffer not be further encroached by accessory structures,
to preserve a defensible record on criterion 4.
