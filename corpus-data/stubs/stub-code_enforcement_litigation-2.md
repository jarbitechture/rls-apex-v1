## Legal Analysis

### Standing and Authority

The County has authority to foreclose a recorded code-enforcement lien under
Fla. Stat. §162.09(3), provided the lien has been recorded for at least
three months and the underlying order has not been satisfied. The 2023-06-14
recording satisfies the three-month threshold as of late 2023. The County's
foreclosure standing is not in serious doubt.

### Practical Considerations

[WORK PRODUCT] — The title search reveals a senior mortgage held by an
out-of-state lender with a stated principal of $187,000. The lender filed a
foreclosure action in March 2023 (Case No. 2023-CA-008842) that has been
inactive since June 2023. Counsel for the lender has not responded to two
inquiries from this office. The lender's first-position lien substantially
limits the County's economic recovery — under standard Florida lien-priority
rules, the County's $52,665 lien would recover only from any surplus after
the senior mortgage and tax certificates are satisfied. Given current
estimated property value of $145,000-$160,000 and a senior mortgage of
$187,000, surplus is unlikely.

The County's policy interest in foreclosure here is therefore not economic
recovery. It is (a) clearing title so the property can be repaired or
demolished without continued accumulation of fines, and (b) signaling to
similarly situated owners that the County will pursue enforcement.

### Demolition Alternative

Manatee County LDC §6.9 authorizes the County to demolish unsecured,
unsafe structures and recover demolition costs as a lien. A demolition
proceeding here would skip the lien-foreclosure step entirely and produce a
clean parcel for resale. Estimated demolition cost: $18,000-$22,000.
Recovery via tax-deed sale after senior mortgage write-off (lender appears
to have abandoned the foreclosure) is moderately probable.

### Procedural Path Comparison

Three viable paths:

1. **Foreclose the code lien.** 8-14 month timeline. Likely no economic
   recovery. Clears title.
2. **Pursue demolition under LDC §6.9.** 4-6 month timeline. County funds
   demolition; cost added to lien. Reduces neighborhood blight.
3. **Coordinate with the senior lender.** Send notice that the County
   intends to demolish and ask the lender to either complete its foreclosure
   or release the lien. 60-day window. Lowest County out-of-pocket cost.
