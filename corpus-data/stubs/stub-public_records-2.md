## Legal Analysis

### Overview of Personnel Records Under Chapter 119

Florida Statute §119.071(4)(a) identifies multiple categories of exempt
information that must be redacted from personnel files before public disclosure:

- Home addresses, telephone numbers, and information identifying the home
  addresses or telephone numbers (§119.071(4)(a)1.) — exemption is permanent
  and mandatory.
- Bank account numbers and similar financial data of county employees
  (§119.071(4)(d)) — permanently exempt.
- Medical history, medical condition, or disability information
  (§119.071(4)(a)8.) — permanently exempt.
- Photographs of employees, unless the employee is an officer
  (§119.071(4)(a)1.) — exempt for non-officer employees.
- Social security numbers in any record (§119.071(4)(a)1.) — permanently exempt.

Additionally, Chapter 119 does not override attorney-client communications
embedded in personnel files (Fla. Stat. §90.502 and §119.071(1)(d)).

### Redaction Log Requirements

For each withheld document and each redaction, HR must prepare a redaction log
that identifies: (a) document description, (b) date, (c) number of pages,
(d) statutory basis for exemption, and (e) whether the document is withheld
in full or partially redacted. Florida AG guidance (AGO 2016-14) requires
sufficient description for the requestor to assess whether to seek judicial
review. Boilerplate redaction logs ("withheld pursuant to Chapter 119") do
not satisfy this standard.
